# Change a user role

This guide will cover how to change a user's role in your workspace.

Note: Only users with the Admin or Lead roles can change another user's role.

  1. Navigate to your workspace
  2. Click on the View Members link in the top right corner of the page
  3. Click on the dropdown role of the user whose role you want to change
  4. Select the new role from the dropdown menu
  5. Click Save Changes at the top of the page
